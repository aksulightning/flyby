Flyby: original source Apache-2.0, https://github.com/aksulightning/flyby
Native runtime:
- RVVM library ce8ca7c00ba4058e5f26811057573b3ff23e9316, MPL-2.0.
  https://github.com/LekKit/RVVM/tree/ce8ca7c00ba4058e5f26811057573b3ff23e9316
  GPL command-line programs are not linked. No upstream source patches.
- libvterm 0.3.3, MIT, copyright 2008 Paul Evans.
  https://www.leonerd.org.uk/code/libvterm/libvterm-0.3.3.tar.gz
- AndroidX, Kotlin, kotlinx.coroutines: Apache-2.0.
- virglrenderer 1.1.1, MIT (includes Mesa utilities); see virglrenderer-MIT.txt.
  https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/virgl/virglrenderer/-/tree/1.1.1
- libepoxy 1.5.10, MIT; see libepoxy-MIT.txt.
  https://github.com/anholt/libepoxy/tree/1.5.10
Guest: Linux 6.18.53 (GPL-2.0-only), OpenSBI 1.9 (BSD-2-Clause),
Alpine Edge riscv64 (20260805 snapshot) including BusyBox (GPL-2.0-only) and musl (MIT).
Every guest package's version, license and exact Alpine source recipe revision
is listed in assets/vm/provenance.json. Full native/core notices are packaged
as assets/*-*.txt. Source/build/provisioning scripts and the redistribution
requirements are in the Flyby repository, docs/licenses.md.
Fresh persistent disks use flybyuser with a first-run password and doas. Its system disk persists; /run and /tmp are temporary.
The Flyby guest filesystem-growth helper and SAF/9P bridge are original Apache-2.0 code.
The growth/input helpers are freestanding; the Vulkan check links the guest's musl and Vulkan loader.

Service Alpine includes OpenRC (BSD-2-Clause). Its full license is available in
OpenRC-BSD-2-Clause.txt. Additional package notices and exact sources are recorded
in vm/provenance.json and the release corresponding-source archive.

Vulkan headers bundled in virglrenderer: Copyright The Khronos Group Inc.,
Apache-2.0. The complete license is in Flyby-Apache-2.0.txt; exact header notices
and sources are retained in the corresponding-source archive.
